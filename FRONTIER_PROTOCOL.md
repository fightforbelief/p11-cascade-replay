# Spend-matched extension, 23 November 2026

This extension follows review of the original retrospective archive study. The MATH and AIME result signs and the question-resampling diagnostics were already known. Before the external analysis, we inspected file schemas, sample rows, scoring code and the notebook service lists. All five 2024 summary files from stanford-futuredata/FrugalGPT revision 2b23e6acbd4b39f71912816640dcc59e45aa4b5e are included. No method is selected using the external comparison results.

1. For every published FrugalGPT cascade operating point compare its test score against (a) the best affordable single service and (b) the best query-independent mixture of direct services at the same realized test cost. Use the notebook's actual cascade training pool (five services, six for OVERRULING) as the primary control. Repeat for all ten reported direct services. Record the sign, the gain in points, and whether changing the control erases a strict advantage. If a budget cannot buy the cheapest direct model, mark it infeasible. Keep the 20 configured budgets, and separately count unique test cost/score coordinates. These are operating points sharing test cases.
2. Construct a deployable comparator using only direct-service training costs and training scores at each author's original nominal budget. Freeze the pair and its probabilities before looking at test outcomes. Report actual average test cost, budget compliance of both policies, and test-score differences. Separately count cases in which the frozen direct policy costs no more than the cascade and matches or exceeds its score. This is a comparison of released policies on their source split; test-envelope results are descriptive upper benchmarks.
3. Attention to small effects: report advantages larger than one score point separately from strict comparisons. Report every dataset and the sign of the comparisons. The released aggregate tables do not identify paired question uncertainty for these external policies.
4. Generalize the original archive replay calculation to the excess quality at its own realized spend over the upper concave envelope of direct policies. Common and question-specific reindexings may move cost and accuracy together. Compute sharp bounds allowing mixtures of admissible alignments. Verify with exhaustively enumerable examples, including an extremum strictly between deterministic alignments. On the original data, include each telemetry-complete recorded-token pair (Nano/Super, Nano/Llama, 14B/32B). Charge the full first response and fallback output tokens at relative prices 1 and 4, price models individually in their own token units. Use only these two direct endpoints in the pair-specific comparison. Omit the 1.5B token and post hoc character arms from this cost experiment. No simulated dollar or latency claim.
