The five unmodified tables are credited to Lingjiao Chen, Matei Zaharia and James Zou and the Stanford Future Data Systems FrugalGPT project. They come from the summary directory of revision 2b23e6acbd4b39f71912816640dcc59e45aa4b5e, under the repository's Apache 2.0 license (included). Tables give historical reported mean scores and costs per query. No current prices are assumed. The examples/FrugalGPT_gen_tradeoff_*2024.ipynb notebooks define the service pools used for training; the authors' src/FrugalGPT/evaluate.py takes the mean of their per-item score and cost fields.
